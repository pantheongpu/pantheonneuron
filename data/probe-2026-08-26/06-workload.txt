=== Environment notes (cost real time to discover) ===
1. SSM RunShellScript executes under /bin/sh, not bash -- `source` fails.
   Use the venv interpreter by absolute path.
2. torch_neuronx import fails with
     FileNotFoundError: 'libneuronpjrt-path'
   unless the venv bin directory is on PATH. Setting the interpreter path
   alone is not sufficient:
     export PATH=/opt/aws_neuronx_venv_pytorch_2_8/bin:/opt/aws/neuron/bin:$PATH
3. First-run compile is ~60s for this trivial graph. Budget compile time
   separately from measurement time on any timed run.
